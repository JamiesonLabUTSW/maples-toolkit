# Content Validation Pattern

Content validation can use multiple perspectives for rubric issues:

- Initial analysis of the issue.
- Critique of other model responses.
- Refined final response.
- Synthesis across responses.

When reproducing this in Codex, simulate distinct perspectives by separating:

- Clinical correctness.
- Educational assessment quality.
- Practical implementation.
- Risk and safety.

Final recommendations should distinguish strong consensus from unresolved uncertainty.
