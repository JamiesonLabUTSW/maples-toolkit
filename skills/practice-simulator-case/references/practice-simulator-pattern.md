# Practice Simulator Pattern

The `/rubrics` practice simulator uses live AI patient behavior. Case design should keep student-facing instructions separate from hidden patient details.

Include:

- Patient demographics and setting.
- Opening line.
- Symptoms and chronology.
- Relevant positives and negatives.
- Emotional tone and communication style.
- What the patient volunteers without prompting.
- What the patient reveals only when asked.
- Boundaries: facts the patient should not invent.
- Expected learner objectives.

For test mode, do not expose diagnosis, hidden findings, or scoring guidance in student-facing text.
